### Canonical Loss Patterns & Root Causes

#### 1. Competing Demand Multi-Order Mapping (Scenario 05: -10.5 pts)
- **Symptom**: When 4 production orders share component demand, the agent successfully pooled quantities and ordered magnets (`CMP-003`) with arrival on `2025-10-26`. However, it only flagged delay against the earliest order deadline (`PO-5001`), omitting an explicit mention of downstream order `PO-5003`.
- **Root Cause**: The prompt did not explicitly instruct the LLM to emit individual delay alerts for *each* affected customer order ID.

#### 2. Customer Order Cross-Tagging (Scenario 03: -3.0 pts)
- **Symptom**: In tight timeline situations, the agent flagged magnet delays accurately, but the qualitative auditor expected direct citation of the customer name ("Nexus Automation / PO-5005").

#### 3. In-Flight PO Evaluation Tolerance (Scenario 02: -2.5 pts)
- **Symptom**: Pre-existing purchase orders (`EXIST-001` through `EXIST-004`) placed weeks prior were evaluated against standard lead times relative to `current_date`.
