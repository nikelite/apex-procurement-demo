Step 1: First Principles Problem Decomposition

Why Naive LLM Wrappers Fail in Manufacturing

Analyzing the fundamental mismatch between stochastic LLMs and manufacturing invariance.

❌ The Failure Modes of Pure Prompting

  • Arithmetic Drift & Hallucination: LLMs make subtle multiplication errors on multi-tier BOM quantities.
  • Policy Bypass under Context Load: Temporal management memos (e.g. MEMO-072 expiry on Sep 30) get diluted when prompts grow.
  • Uncontrolled Non-Determinism: Placing orders with uncertified or unapproved suppliers due to prompt phrasing nuances.

✅ The Forward Deployed Engineering Insight

  • Separate Precision from Reasoning: Calculations & hard policy constraints must be 100% deterministic (0% error rate) in pure Python.
  • Empower LLMs Where They Excel: Multi-vendor split trade-offs, domestic supplier price premiums, audit-ready rationales, and proactive risk alerts.
  • Contract-Driven Outputs: Native Pydantic schema validation for guaranteed database commit safety.

⚙️ How the Pipeline Executes

  • DemandAnalyzer: Explodes BOM, nets inventory, subtracts in-flight POs, and computes material deadlines with zero float errors.
  • PolicyEngine: Pre-filters unapproved or uncertified vendors before the LLM sees them—preventing policy leaks by construction.
  • ProcurementPlanner: Assembles enriched context and prompts the LLM for multi-objective optimization.
  • _plan_deterministic Fallback: Seamless offline execution if cloud API drops.

Step 5: Error Diagnosis & Loss Taxonomy

Diagnosing the Baseline Failure Modes (v1.0)

Documenting failure modes systematically in REP-EVAL-2026-001 rather than making ad-hoc fixes.

Pattern 1: MOQ Excess Holding Risk

In Scenario 10 (78.0%), agent ordered 500 units for 8 needed, but omitted an alert for the $28,536 excess inventory carrying cost.

Pattern 2: Multi-Order Delay Aggregation

In Scenario 05 (89.5%), agent flagged the earliest delivery shortfall, but failed to break down per-order delivery impacts across all 4 POs.

Pattern 3: Implicit Compliance Logging

Documented sole-source justifications inside PO rationale fields, but omitted standalone Section 4 operational risk alerts.
