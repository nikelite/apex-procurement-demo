Problem Understanding

The Procurement Bottleneck at Scale

Manual planning across growing product lines is slow, error-prone, and vulnerable to supplier risks.

🚨 Apex Manufacturing Pain Points

  • Manual BOM Explosion & Netting: Calculating gross demand and subtracting stock/in-flight orders across dozens of lines.
  • Policy & Dated Memo Conflicts: Reconciling base policy (POL-PROC-001) with temporary dated directives (MEMO-041, 072, 085).
  • Multi-Objective Tradeoffs: Domestic preference price premiums vs. strict lead-time schedules vs. MOQ lot rounding.

🎯 FDE Autonomous Solution Goals

  • Zero-Prompt Autonomous Execution: Reads SQLite state snapshot and commits directly to ERP tables.
  • Mathematical & Policy Invariance: 100% precision on netting and Approved Supplier List (ASL) enforcement.
  • Executive Defensibility: Audit-ready rationales and actionable risk alerting for production line continuity.
Architecture Deep-Dive

Hybrid Deterministic-LLM Architecture

Separation of Concerns: Pure Python for Arithmetic & Gatekeeping + LLM for Contextual Reasoning

[ SQLite Database Snapshot ] │ ▼ ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ 1. Deterministic Core (Pure Python) │ │ • DemandAnalyzer: BOM & Netting │ │ • PolicyEngine: ASL & Memo Filter │ └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘ │ Pre-filtered Candidates ▼ ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐ │ 2. Pluggable Reasoning Layer (LLM) │ │ • Multi-vendor split (MEMO-041 50%) │ │ • Defensible PO Rationales & Alerts │ └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘ │ Pydantic Structured Output ▼ [ purchase_orders & alerts Tables ] ──▶ [ Tracer ]

💡 Why Hybrid Design Wins in Production

  • Zero Math Hallucinations: DemandAnalyzer guarantees 0.00% calculation error on gross-to-net math.
  • Guaranteed Policy Gatekeeping: PolicyEngine pre-filters unapproved/uncertified vendors before LLM sees them.
  • Fault-Tolerant Fallback: If API times out, _plan_deterministic() immediately takes over—factory never stops.

Evaluation Harness

100-Point Dual Evaluation Architecture

60 Points Pure Deterministic Scoring + 40 Points Independent Qualitative LLM Audit

📐 Deterministic Evaluation (60 Points Max)

  • Demand Coverage (15 pts): Exact net shortfall fulfillment.
  • MOQ Compliance (10 pts): Valid lot sizes $\ge$ supplier MOQ.
  • Policy & ASL Certs (15 pts): 100% on approved list + ISO/UL certs.
  • Management Memos (10 pts): Magnet 50% cap & PCB rules.
  • Lead Time Calculation (10 pts): Date arithmetic correctness.

🧑‍⚖️ Qualitative LLM-as-a-Judge (40 Points Max)

  • PO Rationale Quality (20 pts): Defensibility, unit price justification, supplier tier citations, and policy cross-referencing.
  • Operational Risk Alerts (20 pts): Schedule delivery delay warnings, Hazmat special handling, and approval triggers.
  • Decoupled Model: Configurable via LLM_JUDGE_MODEL to prevent self-evaluation bias.

Optimization Journey

Data-Driven Hill-Climbing (v1.0 ➔ v1.1)

How structured error analysis in REP-EVAL-2026-001 drove +4.7% score elevation.

📉 v1.0 Baseline Loss Patterns (94.7%)

  • High-MOQ Surplus: Ordering 500 units for 8 demand without capital risk alert (Scenario 10: 78.0%).
  • Multi-Order Contention: Only flagged earliest deadline, missed individual order IDs (Scenario 05: 89.5%).
  • Implicit Compliance: Documented sole-source in PO, but missed standalone alert.

🚀 v1.1 Prompt Registry Resolution (99.4%)

  • Scenario 10 Perfected: 78.0% ➔ 100.0% (+22.0 pts) via [High MOQ Inventory Impact] directive.
  • Scenario 05 Perfected: 89.5% ➔ 100.0% (+10.5 pts) via multi-order deadline breakdown.
