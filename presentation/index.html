Phase 1: First Principles

Why Naive LLM Prompts Fail

Manufacturing requires zero math error and strict policy adherence.

❌ Stochastic LLM Bottlenecks

  • Arithmetic Drift: Subtle float/multiplication errors on BOM math.
  • Policy Leakage: Temporal memos ignored as prompts get long.
  • Supplier Risks: Unapproved/blacklisted vendors selected.

✅ The FDE Engineering Solution

  • Deterministic Core: Pure Python for 0% math & policy error.
  • Cognitive Layer: LLM focused on multi-vendor trade-offs & alerts.
  • Type Safety: Pydantic schemas for guaranteed DB commits.

Phase 3: Evaluation Engine

Dual Evaluation & LLM-as-a-Judge

Automated 100-point scoring framework across 11 diverse test scenarios.

📐 Deterministic Evaluation (60 Points)

  • Demand Coverage (15 pts): Exact net shortfall fulfillment.
  • MOQ Compliance (10 pts): Valid lot sizes $\ge$ supplier MOQ.
  • Policy & ASL Certs (15 pts): 100% approved + ISO/UL certs.
  • Management Memos (10 pts): Magnet 50% split & PCB rules.
  • Lead Time Accuracy (10 pts): Exact date arithmetic.

🧑‍⚖️ Qualitative LLM-as-a-Judge (40 Points)

  • PO Rationale Quality (20 pts): Price defensibility, supplier tier justification, and policy citations.
  • Operational Risk Alerts (20 pts): Delay warnings, Hazmat storage protocols, and approval escalations.
  • Decoupled Model: Configurable via LLM_JUDGE_MODEL to prevent self-evaluation bias.
